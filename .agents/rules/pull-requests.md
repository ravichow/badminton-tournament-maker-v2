# Pull Request Guidelines

- Keep PR descriptions concise, scannable, and focused on key highlights.
- Avoid overly long walls of text; provide brief bullet points for:
  - **Overview**: 1-2 sentence summary of the feature/fix.
  - **Key Highlights**: High-level bullet points of what is changed.
  - **Verification**: Test and build status.
- Ensure commits and PRs are authored and committed by `ravichow`.
